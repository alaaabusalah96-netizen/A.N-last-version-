# Usability & Identity Assessment — AN-IDENTITY-USERS-1

- **Operational Administration**: Administrators can now manage users and their company memberships directly from the "المستخدمون" workspace in the sidebar, providing full oversight of account status and roles.
- **Security Enforcement**: Domain-specific permissions (`USER_READ`/`USER_WRITE`) ensure that only authorized administrative roles can perform user creation, activation/deactivation, or password resets, with full audit trails.
- **Tenant Isolation**: Admin actions are scoped to the administrator's authorized domain, ensuring tenant isolation is maintained during user management.
